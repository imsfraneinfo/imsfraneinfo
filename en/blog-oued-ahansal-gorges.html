Ahansal River Gorges

15 February 2026

The Ahansal gorges were shaped by water over thousands of years. Their cliffs, narrow passages and changing light create a dramatic landscape for hikers.

The region is also home to birds, small mammals, wild plants and traces of traditional Amazigh life.
