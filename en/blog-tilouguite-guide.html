Tilouguite – Gateway to Imsfrane

10 February 2026

Tilouguite is an authentic Amazigh village surrounded by mountains, valleys and traditional farms. It is the main starting point for visiting Imsfrane.

The weekly market, stone houses and local hospitality offer visitors a genuine experience of mountain life.
